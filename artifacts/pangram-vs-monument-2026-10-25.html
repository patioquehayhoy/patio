2 · Mismo texto y medidas con Monument — los 600 con el peso Black real de tu prueba

Your brutalist ally.

Monument is a powerful grotesk typeface inspired by the raw beauty and imposing forms of brutalist architecture. Originally designed to make bold statements, it's become a go-to for impactful design, attention-grabbing titles, and refined typographic compositions that demand presence.

With its major 2025 update, Monument has grown into one of Pangram Pangram's most expansive and adaptable font families. It now includes five distinct widths, each available in nine carefully tuned weights, across both upright and italic styles, offering designers an exceptional range of typographic control.

Whether you're crafting branding systems, editorial layouts, or digital experiences, Monument's versatility and structure make it a workhorse with edge. From ultra-compressed to comfortably wide, it flexes confidently across screens and scales.
